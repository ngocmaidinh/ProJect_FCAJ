Meeting 3

Investigation Series – Learn, Build, Share, Grow

1. Event Objectives

FCAJ Mini Meetup – From Learning to Building, held on October 25, 2026, was a knowledge-sharing event organized by the First Cloud AI Journey community. The event focused on the topic “Cloud Quest – An Interactive AWS Learning Platform for Vietnamese Learners”, helping participants understand how technical knowledge can be transformed into practical products.

Through the presentation by Nguyễn Minh Toàn, participants had the opportunity to explore the research and development journey of Cloud Quest, from the initial idea to the implementation of an interactive learning platform with multiple components.

The main objectives of the event included:

  • Understanding the idea and development goals of the Cloud Quest project.
  • Exploring how to build an interactive AWS learning platform for Vietnamese learners.
  • Learning about interactive 3D models, simulated labs, quizzes, and flashcards.
  • Understanding how to transform acquired knowledge into a technology product with practical value.
  • Encouraging proactive learning, hands-on practice, product development, and knowledge sharing within the community.

2. Speaker and Presentation Topic

  • Nguyễn Minh Toàn – FCAJ Member | PTIT: Cloud Quest – An Interactive AWS Learning Platform for Vietnamese Learners.

3. Key Highlights

3.1. Overview of the Cloud Quest Project

Cloud Quest is a personal project researched and developed by Nguyễn Minh Toàn with the goal of helping learners approach AWS knowledge in a more visual and understandable way.

Instead of relying entirely on theoretical documentation, the project combines different interactive learning methods to help learners acquire knowledge and visualize concepts related to cloud computing.

The project’s main objectives include:

  • Building a platform that helps learners explore AWS knowledge visually.
  • Creating an interactive learning environment through 3D models.
  • Integrating simulated labs to support hands-on learning.
  • Incorporating quizzes and flashcards to reinforce knowledge.
  • Making AWS concepts more accessible to Vietnamese learners.

The project demonstrates that technology learning does not have to be limited to documentation and theoretical materials. It can also be developed into educational products that provide practical value to other learners.

3.2. Research and Development Process of Cloud Quest

One of the highlights of the presentation was the process of developing Cloud Quest from an initial idea into a project containing multiple interactive components.

This process demonstrated the combination of technical knowledge, research capabilities, and product development skills.

The notable components of the project include:

  • Interactive 3D Models: Creating visual environments that allow learners to explore AWS concepts through 3D representations.
  • Simulated Labs: Developing simulated learning activities to help learners understand how AWS services operate.
  • Quizzes: Integrating multiple-choice questions to evaluate understanding and reinforce acquired knowledge.
  • Flashcards: Using digital flashcards to support the review and memorization of important concepts.
  • Learning Content Integration: Combining these components into a structured and interactive learning platform.

Through the development process of Cloud Quest, I realized that building a technology product requires more than theoretical knowledge. Developers also need to identify problems, research solutions, implement features, test functionality, and continuously improve the product.

3.3. From Learning to Building Real-World Products

The topic “From Learning to Building” emphasizes the importance of transforming acquired knowledge into products with practical value.

In technology learning, understanding fundamental concepts is essential. However, the ability to apply that knowledge to solve problems and develop practical products is equally important.

Some valuable lessons from the Cloud Quest development journey include:

  • Proactive Research: Actively exploring new ideas and technologies beyond existing knowledge.
  • Learning Through Practice: Applying acquired knowledge to develop specific features and functionalities.
  • Product Development Mindset: Identifying project objectives, target users, and the practical value of a product.
  • Continuous Improvement: Gradually developing, testing, and improving the product throughout implementation.
  • Knowledge Sharing: Sharing research results and developed products with the community to encourage learning and collaboration.

These lessons demonstrate that effective learning involves not only acquiring knowledge but also applying it to create useful solutions.

4. Knowledge and Skills Acquired

4.1. Technical Knowledge

  • Cloud Computing: Gained a better understanding of how technology products can support learners in exploring AWS concepts.
  • Product Development: Learned about the basic stages of transforming an initial idea into a practical project.
  • Interactive 3D Models: Explored the potential of 3D models in visualizing technical concepts.
  • Practical Simulations: Understood the role of simulated labs in supporting hands-on learning.
  • Learning Support Tools: Learned how quizzes and flashcards can reinforce knowledge and improve learning effectiveness.
  • Testing and Improvement: Recognized the importance of evaluating features and improving a product throughout its development.

4.2. Personal Development Skills

  • Improved proactive thinking when researching and exploring new technologies.
  • Learned to transform ideas into projects with specific functions and objectives.
  • Recognized the importance of combining theoretical knowledge with practical experience.
  • Developed problem-solving skills through the process of building and improving a project.
  • Understood the value of sharing experiences and personal projects with the technology community.

5. Application to My Internship Project

My internship project is titled “Research and Implementation of a Backup and Disaster Recovery System on Amazon Web Services (AWS).”

The project uses Maiora, a fashion e-commerce website, as a practical application to demonstrate cloud infrastructure deployment, data backup, and system recovery.

Although Cloud Quest focuses on developing an AWS learning platform, the knowledge and experience shared during the meetup can be applied to my internship project in several ways:

  • Proactive AWS Research: Continue exploring AWS services, cloud architecture, and deployment procedures through documentation and hands-on practice.
  • Hands-on System Development: Apply acquired knowledge to deploy and configure practical components, including Amazon VPC, EC2, RDS, S3, and AWS Backup.
  • Backup and Recovery Testing: Build practical scenarios to test backup restoration, verify recovered data, and evaluate the ability of the Maiora website to resume operation.
  • Building a Practical Product: Approach the internship project as a practical system that needs to be designed, deployed, tested, and improved rather than treating it only as a theoretical research topic.
  • Documentation and Knowledge Sharing: Document architecture, configurations, implementation steps, test results, and lessons learned to create technical materials that can be shared with others.

These lessons encourage me to actively research, practice, and improve the Backup and Disaster Recovery system instead of stopping at understanding cloud computing concepts.

6. Experience Participating in the Event

Participating in FCAJ Mini Meetup – From Learning to Building on October 25, 2026, gave me an opportunity to gain practical insights into the process of developing a technology project from an initial idea into a product with practical value.

Through Nguyễn Minh Toàn’s presentation about Cloud Quest, I gained a better understanding that learning AWS should not be limited to reading documentation or completing existing labs. It can also be expanded into personal projects that help other learners approach technical knowledge more effectively.

In particular, combining interactive 3D models, simulated labs, quizzes, and flashcards within a single platform demonstrated a creative approach to delivering technical knowledge.

Besides the technical content, I was impressed by the proactive research and product development mindset demonstrated by a member of the FCAJ community. This motivated me to continue developing my internship project, gaining practical experience, and improving my technical and professional skills.

7. Conclusion and Lessons Learned

Through FCAJ Mini Meetup – From Learning to Building on October 25, 2026, I realized that technology learning and research should be closely connected with practical experience and the ability to create useful products.

Nguyễn Minh Toàn’s Cloud Quest project is an example of how knowledge can be transformed into a practical product through research, creativity, and continuous development. The combination of interactive 3D models, simulated labs, quizzes, and flashcards also demonstrates a more engaging approach to learning AWS concepts.

The most important lesson I learned from this meetup is that I should not stop at learning and exploring technical knowledge. Instead, I need to actively apply what I have learned to design, build, test, and improve practical systems.

This is also the spirit that the FCAJ community aims to promote: Not just learning, but also practicing, building, and sharing what we have accomplished with the community.
